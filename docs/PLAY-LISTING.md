## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**EN:**
> El Kaptin is a simple tool for small shops in Egypt — restaurants, takeaways,
> pharmacies, groceries, mini-markets and kiosks — to track their own delivery
> drivers and reconcile cash-on-delivery, with no extra hardware. Just the
> driver's phone.
>
> For drivers:
> • Start your shift and share your live location with your shop while you deliver.
> • See your assigned orders, the cash to collect, and navigate with one tap.
> • Confirm delivery with the customer's code; mark failed deliveries with a reason.
> • Track the cash you're holding and what you've handed over.
>
> Location is used only while you're on shift, to show your shop where you are
> during deliveries, and stops when you end your shift. Battery-friendly: it sends
> a batched update, not a constant stream.
>
> El Kaptin never holds your money — cash goes straight from customer to driver to
> shop; the app just keeps the record straight. Works in Arabic and English.
